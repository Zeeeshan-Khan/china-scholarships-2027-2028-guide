| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 📑 Mandatory Application Checklist (2027 / 2028 Intake)

1. **CSC Online Application Form** (Submitted via Campus China portal).
2. **Notarized Highest Degree Certificate & Transcripts** (English or Chinese translated).
3. **Foreigner Physical Examination Form** (Must be valid within 6 months, signed, stamped, and with photo stamp): [Download Form Guide](https://www.chinascholerships.org/blog/foreigner-physical-examination-form-guide).
4. **Study Plan / Research Proposal** (Minimum 800 words for Master's, 1000+ words for PhD).
5. **Two Recommendation Letters** (From Associate Professors or Full Professors).
6. **Certificate of Non-Criminal Record (Police Clearance)** (Issued within 6 months).
7. **English Proficiency Certificate (EPC)** (IELTS is **NOT mandatory** if your previous degree was taught in English).
8. **Professor Acceptance Letter** (Recommended for CSC Type B): [Download Email Templates](https://www.chinascholerships.org/document-templates).
